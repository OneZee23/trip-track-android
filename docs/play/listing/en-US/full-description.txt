TripTrack is a road-trip diary. Record a drive and return to its route, photos and numbers later.

Record manually or enable automatic recording. With your permission, recording continues in the background when the app is closed or not in use. See distance, duration, speed and elevation, pause a recording and resume when you are ready.

Keep photos and notes with a trip. Add checkpoints along the way, review the route on the map and replay the drive. Export a route as GPX or trip data as CSV.

Your Atlas brings recorded roads together on one map. Add vehicles to the Garage and keep their trips and mileage together. Levels and badges mark the distance you have travelled.

Recording works without an internet connection. Loading maps and using cloud features need a connection. You can use the diary without an account. Cloud Sync is off by default and can be enabled in settings.

TripTrack is free and has no ads.
